What it actually does, in plain English

KnightTrader Poly is a desktop app for Windows 11 and Apple Silicon Macs. It runs a local assistant against the Polymarket account whose keys you paste in. You watch the desk. You can stop it any time.

1

You fund Polymarket with pUSD

That balance is the only money this app can use. Start small. The assistant is told not to size up until the account is 50% ahead of where it started.

2

You paste four Polymarket values

API key, secret, passphrase, and the wallet private key. They stay in an encrypted file on this PC. If the pasted API key is rejected, the app derives CLOB credentials from the private key and uses those for the account read.

3

You install the app and add a Nous key

Download the installer for your system and run it. Nous Portal needs a one-time ~$5 credit top-up, paid to Nous, before it will issue an AI key. After that the app picks a free model.

4

The assistant checks every 5 minutes

Each pass uses Polymarket CLOB v2. It scans crypto 5-minute up/down markets, scores the book, and either does nothing or buys the exchange minimum of 5 shares. Markets that expire within those five minutes are included only if the same edge test passes.

5

You keep the kill switch

Stop the schedule in the app, or quit the app. Closing the window leaves it in the tray. Your pUSD stays on Polymarket. Revoke the API key there if you want the app to lose access.

What it does not do

A few limits, so you know what you are installing.

×

A 5-minute crypto contract can finish against you. The 5-share cap until +50% is an instruction, not a promise. You can lose the stake.

×

Funds stay in your Polymarket account. The app never sees your debit card, your bank login, or a withdrawal address.

×

New orders are rejected from a blocked network. The How-To names which free Proton countries Polymarket allows. If the geoblock check still says blocked, the app will not open a new order.

×

The assistant needs this PC awake and online. The How-To tab shows how to stop Windows and macOS from sleeping.

Questions people actually ask

Does this work on a Mac?

Yes, on Apple Silicon. Open the .dmg, drag the app into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time if macOS blocks an unidentified developer. Intel Macs are not in this build.

Can I run this next to KnightTrader BloFin?

Yes. Poly uses its own data folder, its own ports, and its own updater. Installing it does not replace BloFin, and updating one does not update the other.

What do I paste in?

The Polymarket CLOB API key, secret, and passphrase, plus the wallet private key for that account. Anyone with their own four values can point the app at their own account. The pasted API key is tried first. If Polymarket rejects it, the app derives credentials from the private key for the account read.

Do I need a VPN?

Only if your own network is blocked. Free Proton usually lands in the Netherlands, Mexico, Canada, Singapore, Romania, Poland, or Japan. Use Mexico or Romania. Netherlands and Japan are usable for this app only when https://polymarket.com/api/geoblock says blocked: false. Do not use Poland, Singapore, or a generic Canada server.

Where do the keys go?

They stay on your machine, in an encrypted local store, and in the assistant’s local folder when you install it. They are not uploaded to KnightTrader.

Is the $5 Nous cost recurring?

Not from KnightTrader. Nous Portal requires a one-time ~$5 credit top-up, paid to Nous, before it will create an API key. After that the app selects free models. You pay Nous again only if you switch to a paid model or Nous changes its billing.

Is this a subscription?

No. $39 once for the app, plus the separate Nous top-up. There is no KnightTrader membership check.

How do I get an older build?

Use the KnightTrader Poly releases page.

Can I lose money?

Yes. These are short crypto up/down contracts. A wrong window loses the stake. Only deposit pUSD you can lose in full, and check the desk.
